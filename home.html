How it works

Three questions. One clear answer.

1

Pick or say your food

Choose from 80 foods — fruits, vegetables, dairy, snacks, pickles and dried produce — or search and speak its name.

2

Storage, time and travel

Room, cold store or fridge. Three days to three months. Local market, truck, courier or export.

3

Get your pack

See what to buy, what to avoid, how long it lasts and roughly what it costs. Share the spec sheet with your supplier.

What you get

Everything you need to pack it right

Built from published post-harvest research, explained in words a farmer or shopkeeper can act on today.

The right pack, in plain words

Mesh bag, perforated film, foil pouch or vented jar — matched to how your food breathes and spoils.

Speak in your language

English, Hindi or Marathi, with voice search. Advice can be read aloud too.

Warnings before losses

Chilling injury, ethylene damage, too-long storage and food-safety rules flagged before they cost you.

Spec sheet for suppliers

A printable sheet with a QR code. Send it on WhatsApp so your supplier gives you exactly the right material.

Cost and greener options

Rough cost in rupees for each bag, pouch or crate, plus a more eco-friendly alternative.

Expert mode

Respiration rates, target O₂/CO₂ and the film oxygen transmission your bag needs.
