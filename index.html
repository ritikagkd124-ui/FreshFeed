About FreshFeed

Less food lost. More money in the farmer’s pocket.

A large share of fruits and vegetables in India spoil after harvest — often because they are packed in the wrong bag, sealed when they need to breathe, or chilled when they cannot take the cold.

FreshFeed turns decades of post-harvest science into simple, local-language advice. We match each food’s breathing rate, ideal temperature and humidity, and gas needs to a pack you can actually buy nearby.
